Privacy notice

Spatial Assembly AR · last updated 14 October 2026

In short: no account, no cookies, no tracking and no advertising. The camera picture is processed on your device and never leaves it. When the app hits an error it sends a short technical report, described below, so the fault can be fixed.

Who is responsible

Outthinkx, Luca De Simoni, is responsible for this app and this website. Contact: github.com/lucadesimoni.

If you use the app on another organisation's server or inside another organisation's website, that organisation runs it and is responsible for it. Its own privacy notice applies.

Camera and motion sensors

Augmented reality needs the camera and, on some devices, the motion sensors. The browser asks your permission first. The picture and the sensor readings are processed only in your browser, on your device. They are not uploaded, stored or seen by us. Recognition (OpenCV and ONNX Runtime) runs locally from files served by this website.

What is stored on your device

WhatWhy
Your notes on steps and parts (spatial-ar.annotations.v1)So they are still there next time.
A summary of the last AR session, without images (spatial-ar:last-ar-report)So a fault can still be reported after the app was closed.
The app's own files (service worker cache)So the app opens quickly and works offline.

This stays in your browser. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Diagnostics log

The app keeps a log of what it did in the current session. It leaves your device only if you choose to save or copy it and send it to someone. If you attach camera frames to it, the log contains those pictures, so look at it before you send it.

Error reports

When the app hits an error, it sends a short report to this website (/api/errors): at most five per visit, and the same error only once. A report contains:

  • the error message and where in the app's code it happened;
  • the app version;
  • the page path, without anything after it in the address;
  • your browser's user agent (browser, version, operating system and device model);
  • how long after opening the page it happened.

We do not store your IP address with a report, and a report contains no pictures, nothing you typed and no identifier for you or your device. Reports are used only to find and fix faults, which is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection). They are kept in the server's logs for at most 30 days.

Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data about you, ask for its processing to be restricted, object to it, and ask for a copy. Contact us at the address above. Error reports hold no identifier, so we can usually not tell which, if any, came from you. You may also complain to a data protection authority. In Switzerland that is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). In the EU it is the authority where you live or work.
